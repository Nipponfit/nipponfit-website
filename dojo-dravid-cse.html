Karate Classes at the Dravid Centre for Sports Excellence, Yelahanka

A national-standard sports facility in Yelahanka, shared with some of India's leading athletes, and our dojo for north Bengaluru.

About this dojo

Nippon Karate Club has its own dedicated floor, fully set up as a dojo, inside the Padukone-Dravid Centre for Sports Excellence in Yelahanka. It is the closest of our dojos for families in Yelahanka, Jakkur, Hebbal, Sahakar Nagar, Thanisandra and Devanahalli.

This is where much of our competition preparation happens, and where we hosted Tokyo Olympic bronze medallist Ugur Aktas for an elite kumite seminar, a first for India. Beginners train here too, on the same WKF-aligned syllabus as every Nippon Fit dojo.

Classes are taught by Renshi Pooja Shri Shetty, an international karateka with a Tokyo Olympics 2020 standing of 181, together with Jeevan J.
