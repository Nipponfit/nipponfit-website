Karate Classes at The Koramangala Club

Nippon Karate Club inside The Koramangala Club, 6th Block. Open to club members and non-members alike.

About this dojo

Our Koramangala dojo brings the full Nippon Karate Club syllabus to central Bengaluru. It suits families in Koramangala, HSR Layout, Indiranagar, Ejipura and BTM Layout who would rather not cross the city for a good karate class.

Training follows the same WKF-aligned syllabus and grading standard as all our dojos: kihon, kata and kumite, from white belt upwards. Students who show promise can move up to the club's Elite competition squad.

You do not need to be a member of The Koramangala Club to train with us. Members and non-members are both welcome.

What parents ask about this dojo

Do I need to be a member of The Koramangala Club?

No. Club members and non-members can both join. Message us and we will tell you the fee that applies to you.

Where exactly is it?

The Koramangala Club, CA-17, 6th Cross Road, 6th Block, Koramangala, Bengaluru 560095. Tap Directions above to open it in Google Maps.

What age can my child start?

From four years old. Adults are welcome to start at any age.

What are the class timings?

Is the first class free?

Yes. The first class is free, with no obligation afterwards.
